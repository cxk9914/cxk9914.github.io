The problem

Rejections are not "the cost of doing business".

In Saudi Arabia and the UAE, cash-on-delivery orders get refused at the door far more often than sellers expect. Every refused order is paid for twice.

Orders a month is where it really hurts

A rejected order doesn't just cost you the product. You eat the two-way freight, the support time spent chasing the buyer, and the collection fee you never earn. Add it up and one refusal burns roughly $89.

At 500 orders a month, that is money leaving the business every single month — quietly, and mostly to buyers who simply changed their mind.

The fix is not logistics. It is talking to the buyer — in Arabic — before the courier shows up.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The Basic and Pro kits are scripts, spreadsheets and PDFs you use directly. The Automation kit asks only that you can import a JSON file into n8n and set a few environment variables. The included placeholder checklist tells you exactly which values to swap.

Do I need a GPU?

No. The AI compliance review runs on a cloud LLM with three environment variables, or you can flip the built-in skip-AI-review switch. The mock demo suite runs entirely on your machine — no GPU, no API keys.

The scripts and templates are written for Saudi Arabia and the UAE (Saudi / Gulf Arabic). The workflow itself is country-agnostic — English and Southeast Asia language packs are on the roadmap.
